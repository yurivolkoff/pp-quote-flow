    View 4 — the quote document. Four states, an unresolvable link, and the two optional modules.
  • A1 — The reference number is the h1, with a real issued date and a real expiry date. Never "subject to change" — procurement cannot cite a quote it cannot name.
  • A2 — "Prepared for" names the requester, because the person reading this is often the approver the link was forwarded to. It gains a company line once "Add details" is used.
  • A3 — Calculate delivery asks the shipping question after the number exists rather than before it. Choosing an option replaces the "calculated at checkout" row, recomputes the total, switches the trust line, stamps the document, and offers a re-send rather than sending a second email unasked.
  • A4 — Add details collects company, phone and need-by after capture. Phone carries its reason. A need-by date earlier than the arrival date is answered, not accepted silently.
  • A5 — Both modules keep the quote number. Delivery and contact details are additive and leave the quoted goods price untouched; a configuration change is what makes a new number. A dated revision stamp under the reference records what was added.
  • A6 — The expired state never silently re-prices: the table dims, a "prices as of" caption appears, and the CTA becomes "Get an updated quote" under a new number.
  • A7 — Print calls the real print stylesheet: the module triggers and forms are hidden, their results are not. One template, so the page and the PDF cannot drift.
